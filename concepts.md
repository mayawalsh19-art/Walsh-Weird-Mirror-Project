1. Melted Mirror

Input: Webcam and viewer's movement 
Changes: The viewer's reflection begins to melt, stretch, and drip when they move
How do they know?: They will see their own body distort immediately when they move. 

2. Don't Blink

Input: Webcam and face/eye detection
Changes: The image becomes strange while the viewer looks at it, but resets or changes when they blink or look away. 
How do they know?: They notice the image reacting specifically when they stop looking at it. 

3. Personal Spotlight

Input: Webcam/body position 
Changes: A spotlight follows the viewer while everything outside their silhouette becomes dark or distorted. 
How do they know?: Walking left or right visibly moves the spotlight with them

4. Motion Echo

Input: Webcam and movement 
Changes: Every movement leaves delayed copies of the viewer behind, creating a trail of their past positions. 
How do they know?: They wave an arm and see several versions of that movement following behind. 

5. Freeze Me 

Input: Webcam/motion detection 
Changes: Moving causes the reflection to distort, but standing completely restores a normal image. 
How do they know?: They discover that by becoming still it makes their reflection clearer. 

6. Sound Face 

Input: Microphone/webcam
Changes: Their reflection distorts based on how loudly they speak. Quiet sounds cause small ripples and louder sounds create extreme distortion. 
How do they know?: They hear themselves make a sound and see their face/body react. 

7. Pixel Escape

Input: Webcam/movement
Changes: Pixels around the viewer scatter away from parts of their body as they move. 
How do they know?: Waving their hand appears to physically push pieces of the image away. 

8. Invisible Body 

Input: Webcam/movement and body detection. 
Changes: Parts of the viewer disappear when they move and slowly reappear when they stop. 
How do they know?: They immediately see pieces of their body vanish as they begin moving. 

9. Color Thief

Input: Webcam/viewer position 
Changes: Different areas of the screen remove or exaggerate colors in the viewer's reflection. 
How do they know?: Walking around the frame visibly changes the colors of their clothes, skin, and surroundings. 

10. Glitch Zone

Input: Webcam/distance from camera. 
Changes: The closer the viewer gets, the more corrupted and glitches their reflection becomes. Far away, the image appears normal. 
How do they know?: They can physically step forward and backward and watch the glitch intensify following their distance.
